Beyond the Resume

Hi, I'm Maya Mukabe — an author and student at Grambling State University, where I'm double majoring in Computer Information Systems and Business Management.

I'm passionate about the intersection of technology, business, and human potential, with a particular interest in pursuing a career in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Machine Learning.
I enjoy exploring how data and emerging technologies can uncover meaningful insights and solve real-world problems.

Through my academic and professional experiences, I've worked on projects involving machine learning, AI-powered contract analysis, data analysis, and the Internet of Things (IoT). I'm also interested in how artificial intelligence influences human learning, attention, and behavior.

Beyond technology, I'm a writer and published author of The Mind, a book exploring mindset, imagination, and personal growth. Writing allows me to express my creativity, share ideas, and inspire others.

I'm also passionate about entrepreneurship and using technology to transform industries such as agriculture. My long-term goal is to build businesses that create opportunities & empower communities.
